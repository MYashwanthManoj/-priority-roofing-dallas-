How we build it right

The Priority difference is in the details — an 8-step installation process that protects your roof for decades.

  1. 01

    Tear Off

    The first thing we do is tear off all existing layers of shingles.

  2. 02

    Decking

    We replace up to 3 sheets of decking for no additional cost. Bad or rotted decking is replaced and invoiced to your insurance company for the difference.

  3. 03

    Underlayment

    The synthetic underlayment we lay down provides a 100% waterproof barrier prior to the shingles being installed — significantly better than industry-standard #15 felt paper, which is NOT waterproof.

  4. 04

    Starter Strip

    Starter strip shingles around the perimeter of every roof, with a lifetime warranty and a 110 mph wind warranty.

  5. 05

    Valley Protection

    Ice & Water Shield by GAF — a rubber membrane that won't tear or rip. Peel-and-stick, self-sealing around nails.

  6. 06

    DuraFlo Flashings

    DuraFlo pipe flashings around all plumbing pipes — a 35-year warranty on the flashings. If it leaks, they replace it.

  7. 07

    Ventilation

    All necessary equations are performed to ensure the proper NFVA so your roof and home can breathe and avoid malfunctions.

  8. 08

    Yard Cleanup

    Once finished, we go over the entire yard with a magnetic toolbar.
